Frequently Asked Questions

Arizona AC & HVAC Answers

Everything you need to know about keeping your home cool in the desert heat.

Why is my AC running constantly and not cooling below 78°F?

In extreme Arizona summers (110°F+), standard AC units are designed to maintain a 20° to 25° difference between outdoor and indoor temperatures. If your system is failing to keep up, it could be caused by a dirty air filter, low refrigerant levels (a leak), a failing capacitor, dirty condenser coils, or leaky ductwork. Peterson Air Conditioning can perform an exact diagnostic to restore peak cooling.

How often should I have my air conditioner serviced in Mesa?

Because Arizona air conditioning units run nearly year-round under harsh heat and dust conditions, we strongly recommend a comprehensive tune-up **at least once per year (preferably in spring before summer heat waves arrive)**. If you have a heat pump that provides both heating and cooling, bi-annual checks (spring and fall) are ideal.

Should I repair or replace my aging air conditioner?

A good rule of thumb is the "$5,000 rule": multiply the age of the equipment by the repair cost. If the total exceeds \$5,000, and your unit is over 10–12 years old (or uses phased-out R-22 Freon), upgrading to a new high-efficiency SEER2 unit often pays for itself in energy savings and warranty protection.

We offer 24/7 priority emergency dispatch across Mesa, Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, and Phoenix. During extreme summer heat emergencies, we prioritize complete cooling outages to protect families, elderly residents, and pets. Call us directly at (480) 555-0199 for immediate dispatch.
